# Neutrón (formerly Home Reps): notes for Claude

- The app is called **Neutrón** (written with the accent). Its logo is an N with one orbit and a particle (`icons/icon.svg`, `LOGO_SVG` in `index.html`; PNGs in `icons/`). The repository, file names and the `homereps.` storage prefix keep the old name on purpose: don't rename them.

- This web app is the prototype of a native app (React Native + Expo, iOS and Android, English UI). Plans and decisions live in `docs/` (start with `docs/fase-1-arquitectura.md`); team docs are in Spanish.
- Single-file app: `index.html` holds all CSS, HTML and JS. Keep it dependency-free with no build step, so it runs from GitHub Pages and by opening the file. The one exception: MediaPipe pose, lazy-loaded only when camera counting starts. `loadPose()` tries the copy in `vendor/mediapipe/` first (bundle, SIMD wasm and the lite model; see its README), then jsDelivr and unpkg (opened as a file it skips the local copy). Publish the `vendor/` files with the artifact and include them in the Netlify zip. `camError()` says in plain words why the camera can't count (blocked, framed view, no camera, busy, tracking didn't load) with a Try again button; tap counting is the fallback.
- Data tables live near the top of the script: `EX_RAW` (exercises: id, name, muscle groups, equipment, level 1-3, dose, unit, steps), `STRETCH_RAW`, `MEALS`, `RECIPES` (Recipes tab; videos live in `media/` as .mp4 + .webm with a .jpg poster), `PLAN` (weekday split) and `GROUPS`.
- Muscle shapes are SVG paths in `FRONT_PATHS` / `BACK_PATHS` (viewBox 200x430). Paths marked `1` are mirrored to the other side.
- Colors are CSS variables on `:root` with dark-mode overrides; use the tokens, not literal colors.
- State is saved in `localStorage` under the `homereps.` prefix (profile, schedule, done, tab, mode, onboarding, health, plan, points, challenges, habits, alarm, alarmState, meals, log, session, bestStreak, game, rivals, master, masterReq, hints, hintsOff, hero, bodyMode, subs, rest, waist, photoLast, camTest; `survey` and `whyAsked`; `survey` is legacy and no longer read, and `points` is only read once to seed `game`). Keep old keys readable when changing data shapes.
- Plan safety rules live in the `safety-core` block of `index.html` (pure functions, no DOM). Every plan draft, AI or local, must pass `validatePlan`. Run `node --test tests/*.test.mjs` after touching that block.
- Every open starts on the Challenge/Training picker. Challenge asks nothing (high-impact challenges need the `safety` flow, which asks age if missing). Training's first use runs the 5-screen `gym` flow (age, goal, experience/days/minutes, time and sleep, safety) that builds a starter week shown as a 4-week block in the Plan tab (`v-sched`). Day one of the plan is the sign-up day: training days come from `trainDaysFrom(date, daysPerWeek)` (same rhythm rotated to start today) and the Plan tab lists the next 7 days from today. The daily battery (sleep first, shown on Today and at the top of the Challenge hub; a red battery pre-selects the easier challenge version) and block week live in the `habits-core` block (pure functions, tested in `tests/habits.test.mjs`); see `docs/plan-y-habitos.md`. Exercises with gym equipment only show for `track: 'gym'` users.
- The app has two modes picked on every open: Training (the tabs) and Challenge (`v-challenge` hub plus the full-screen `#arena`). Challenge catalog and progression rules live in the `challenge-core` block (pure functions, tested in `tests/challenges.test.mjs`); see `docs/retos-y-onboarding-corto.md`.
- The alarm is stopped on camera: `ALARM_MOVES` in `alarm-core` (push-ups, squats, sit-ups, jumping jacks, pull-ups) with Easy/Medium/Hard reps (`ALARM_LEVELS`; starting level from training experience via `alarmLevelFor`) or your own number with − / +. `POSE_VIEW` says where the phone goes (side-on or in front). Tapping only appears if the camera fails or hasn't counted a rep after 30 s. The alarm settings have a camera check (`camCheckHtml`): "Test it" runs a 3-rep practice (`openAlarm(date, 'cam')`) and saves the result in `camTest`; "Try the whole alarm" is `openAlarm(date, 'ring')`. Practice runs never give XP or touch `alarmState`. Moves in your injury list are locked, high-impact ones (jumping jacks) wait for the health check, and only a real health risk (`alarmGentle()`) turns the task into an easy minute. Each move needs `REP_RULES`, `CUES`, `POSE_VIEW` and `GAME.limits.rep` (checked by `tests/alarm.test.mjs`).
- Phase 3: camera rep counting rules live in `pose-core` and wake-up alarm rules in `alarm-core` (pure functions, tests in `tests/pose.test.mjs` and `tests/alarm.test.mjs`); see `docs/fase-3-alarma-camara.md`. Camera frames never leave the device; meal photos go to Claude only with the separate tick box.
- Duels (challenge a friend) use the published page's shared `db` (`duels` collection: challengeId, scaled, createdAt, from/to {uid, score, medal, video asset id, at}, status) plus `assets` for attempt videos and `user` for ids and names. Store only user ids, never names. Nothing is shared without the tick box; videos are recorded with MediaRecorder or added from the gallery and shrunk on the device to under 20 MB. Without these capabilities (GitHub Pages, local file) the Duels section is hidden. See `docs/duelos.md`.
- "Lose belly fat" goal (`GYM_GOALS` `belly`): saved as `goal:'lose'` with `focus:'belly'`, so every fat-loss rule applies; the local plan adds a core move to every session. Be honest: fat can't be lost from one spot. `bellySheet()` explains what works (after the questionnaire and in Help), `quiz-belly` busts the crunches myth, and the waist (`waist` key, `waistCard()` in Progress → Charts) is the measure. Waist tracking is hidden for anyone whose answers suggested eating concerns.
- Progress photos (Progress → Photos, `drawPhotos()`, `photoCam()`): front and side shots stored only on the phone in IndexedDB (`neutron-photos`, `neutron-photos-demo` in demo), never in `localStorage` or the shared `db`. The camera shows the first photo of that pose as a faint overlay so every shot matches the same spot and framing; there's a timer and a gallery fallback. Two or more photos give a before/now slider, a stats strip (days trained between them, waist change) and "Share before / after" (a composite made on the phone, shared only on tap). A new photo is due every `PHOTO_EVERY` days (the Photo day quest; `photoLast` key). Photos are blurred until tapped and, like waist tracking, hidden for anyone whose answers suggested eating concerns (`photosAllowed`). Deleting all data also deletes the photo database.
- Rest between sets: `restOf(id, goal, planRest)` (your own setting in the `rest` key, then the plan's `restSec`, then `restFor` by goal and equipment). The workout shows it per exercise with − / + and a live countdown on the gap between the two sets (`startTimer(sec, gap, label)`); Today shows it under each exercise.
- Live workout logging (`#session` overlay: sets, previous, suggestion, PRs, rest timer, plates, warm-up), the Progress tab (streak heatmap, strength, volume and battery charts), the Body recovery map and the streak chip use the `training-core` block (pure functions, tests in `tests/training.test.mjs`). Tabs are Today, Plan, Body, Progress, Food; Recipes opens from Food. See `docs/comparativa-mercado.md`.
- Every exercise and challenge move has a looping SVG animation: the rig (2-bone IK, pose blending, timing) is the `anim-core` block (pure, tested in `tests/anim.test.mjs`); poses live in `ANIMS` (one entry per `EX_RAW` id, side view unless `v:'front'`), drawn by `animScene` and mounted automatically for any `svg[data-anim]` (use `animSvg(id)`). The first two muscle groups glow. They show in How to (steps folded away), exercise cards, Today, the workout, challenge intros and the alarm. Add an `ANIMS` entry when you add an exercise.
- XP, levels and points: every reward goes through `gameEvent()`, which applies the `game-core` rules (`GAME` holds every number: XP per activity, daily caps, level curve, points per level, anti-cheat limits; tests in `tests/game.test.mjs`). Never add XP or points any other way. The `game` key holds the XP ledger and the append-only points ledger. Challenge XP feeds the same level. After changing `game-core`, run `node scripts/sync-game-core.mjs` so the Supabase functions use the same rules.
- Rankings by sector (gym, calisthenics, home bodyweight) and private leagues use the shared `db` (`players/<uid>` with nickname, sector, XP and league codes; `leagues/<code>`). Only nicknames are shown, never account names. Someone in your leagues passing you triggers a notice every time. Tips and quizzes are `LEARN_DEFAULT` plus rows in the `content` collection. A quiz (`type:'ab'`) has 2 or 3 options (`a`, `b`, optional `c`), each an `ANIMS` id (e.g. `pushup-sag`, `squat-half`, `plank-pike`, `lift-good`, `lift-round`, `lift-far`) or a `video`; the item can also carry a `video` (+ `poster`) shown above the options. Every item has a short `topic`. One item is the tip of the day (`tipOfDay`: an item whose `day` is today, otherwise one per day in turn), shown on the Daily tip tile. Videos go in `media/` and must be published with the artifact's `files`. Instead of a real video, an item can have a `clip`: a short animated video made of `ANIMS` steps with captions and a ✓/✕ mark (`CLIPS` key or inline list of `{anim, text, mark, ms}`), played by `clipStart`; quizzes show their clip after you answer. Dated items are a surprise until their day (`learnList` hides future ones; the Daily tips sheet shows a calendar). A scheduled task adds new daily tips to the shared `content` collection.
- The MÁSTER section (coach Dani's personal plans) is a secret: nothing in the app may hint at it. Its unlock and texts live only in the `master-core` block; how it unlocks is documented in `dev/master-privado.md` only (never in README or docs). Visible text says "a coach", not Dani's name.
- Backend: `supabase/` (migration with row level security, `seed.sql`, `submit-event` and `delete-account` functions). The client never writes XP, points or flags; `submit-event` validates events with `_shared/submit.mjs` + `_shared/game-core.mjs` (tests in `tests/submit.test.mjs`). Check the database rules with `scripts/test-supabase.sh`.
- Visual first, little text: one job per screen. Today is the day card, then the home menu (`homeMenu`: one row of round icons with a one-word label and the status on the icon itself: quest ring, battery ring or a dot to check in, tip dot, alarm time or muted when off; Training shows quests, battery, hero, tip, ranks, alarm; Challenge shows battery, quests, duels, ranks; Help is only behind the ? in the header and meals live in Food), then the exercises; the personal plan card sits at the bottom. The week strip has no boxes (chosen day is a pill, today in accent). First-time hints are a slim one-line strip with ×. In Challenge the rank card is one compact strip (medal counts beside the rank), today's challenge shows name and target, and locked levels fold into one slim row each. Long tabs are split into sub-tabs (`SUBS`, `subTabs()`, sections marked `data-sec="tab:key"`, `applySub()`): Plan (week, alarm), Food (today, ideas, recipes, my answers), Progress (streak, level, charts), My hero (stats, muscles), Challenge (challenges, my battles). Quests and duels open as sheets. Every section has an icon (`ICONS`, drawn with `ico()` / `tile()`), a one-line hint the first time it's opened (`hintText`, short, personalised by level, track and plan; shown by `placeHint()`), and a Help center (the ? in the header; `HELP` topics with steps and a button that jumps there via `jumpTo`). When you add a section, add its icon, hint and Help topic. Never mention the MÁSTER in hints or Help.
- RPG hero (Body → My hero): `hero-core` (pure, tested in `tests/hero.test.mjs`) turns everything done (logged sets, Today ticks, challenges, camera alarms; gathered by `heroActs()`) into a level per muscle (tiers bronze, silver, gold, legend), six stats (Strength, Endurance, Core, Power, Discipline, Balance) and a class. `heroCheck()` pops a level-up when a muscle or stat goes up. Daily quests (`questList`, shown by `questsSheet`) open from the Quests tile.
- Notices use `pushBanner()` (a phone-style banner that drops from the top, also sent as a system notification when allowed) with the loud Neutrón sound `neutronBurst()` (Web Audio: boom, crash, rising zap, echo, through a limiter; every tap keeps audio unlocked). `media/neutron-alert.wav` is the same sound as a file, for setting it as the Android notification sound. Demo mode for promo videos (Help → Demo for videos): `demoEnter()` backs up every `homereps.*` key to `homereps_demo_backup`, loads a full example account (plan, 12-day streak, level 6, hero, league "Office crew" with fake players) and `demoLeave()` restores it. In demo, nothing is written to the shared `db`; `DEMO_NOTES` fire banners now or after a delay (Ariete passed you, alarm, level up, daily tip, duel, streak).
- Installable web app: `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js` (network first, cache fallback, notification clicks) and `icons/`. The service worker only registers over https or localhost in a top-level window (never inside the Claude artifact frame). Bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` when the cached file list changes. Real phone notifications go through `sysNotify()` (service worker when installed). How to install: README, "Put it on your phone".
- Signature look: `--accent-2` (hot pink) is the second accent that `--accent` fades into on the hero surfaces only (Start workout, the Challenge card on the picker, today's challenge, Share my week, the reactor). The day card, streak card and rank card are always dark (`--arena`) with an accent glow, in both themes. The picker shows the live logo (`logoMark()`: the particle runs round its orbit).
- The reactor (`reactorSvg`, `reactorSet`) on today's day card: the ring and core charge with the share of exercises ticked, and each one done adds a particle to its orbit; rest days show a slow core. Ticking the last one fires `criticalMass()` (shockwave, `neutronBurst()`, confetti, "Share my week"). `shareWeek()` draws a 1080 × 1920 story of the week (`weekRecap()`: days trained, streak, XP or exercises, level, hero class) on the phone and shares it through `shareBlob()` (share sheet, the artifact's `downloads` capability, or a download). The demo pre-ticks three of today's exercises so the reactor glows on camera.
- Motion uses the `--spring` / `--out` easing tokens; JS checks `RM()` and CSS has a reduced-motion rule, so keep both working when adding animation.
- Test changes at phone width (about 400px) in light and dark mode.
